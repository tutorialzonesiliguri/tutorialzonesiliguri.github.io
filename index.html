About Tutorial Zone

Personal Attention Over Crowded Classrooms

Tutorial Zone is a dedicated coaching center in Hakimpara, Siliguri, West Bengal, specializing in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (PCMB) for Classes 9 to 12, alongside JEE (Mains & Advanced) and NEET UG preparation.

Founded in April 2019 and personally taught by Arkajyoti Bhattacharjee—who cleared JEE Mains on his first attempt—every batch is capped at 10–12 students so each learner gets genuine individual attention in a friendly, approachable environment. Built around clear fundamentals, structured Daily Practice Problems (DPPs), and regular board and entrance test series, Tutorial Zone guides students toward top academic performance, including recent CBSE Class 10 scores above 95%.

Frequently Asked Questions

Direct answers for parents and students looking for small-batch Classes 9–12 PCMB, JEE, or NEET coaching in Siliguri.

Which classes, subjects, and boards does Tutorial Zone teach?

Tutorial Zone teaches Classes 9 to 12 across CBSE, ICSE, and ISC boards in Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and Biology (PCMB), along with dedicated coaching for JEE (Mains & Advanced) and NEET UG.

What is the batch size at Tutorial Zone, Siliguri?

Every batch is strictly capped at 10 to 12 students. Small batch sizes allow every learner to ask doubts freely in a friendly environment and receive personal answer-sheet feedback instead of getting lost in a crowded classroom.

Who teaches the classes at Tutorial Zone?

Batches at Tutorial Zone are personally taught by founder Arkajyoti Bhattacharjee, who cleared JEE Mains on his first attempt and has been mentoring Siliguri students since April 2019.

Does Tutorial Zone provide Daily Practice Problems (DPPs) and test series?

Yes. Students receive custom Daily Practice Problems (DPPs), chapter-wise revision worksheets, and timed mock test series for Class 10 and 12 boards (CBSE, ICSE, ISC) as well as JEE and NEET, complete with detailed one-on-one paper evaluation.

What kind of results have Tutorial Zone students achieved?
